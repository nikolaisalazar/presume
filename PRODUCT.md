# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Presume serves job seekers—especially technically minded users—who want direct control over a polished resume without manually tuning layout after every edit. They work inside the finished document, need trustworthy local persistence and export, and may use advisory review evidence to guide their own revisions.

## Product Purpose

Presume is a local-first, direct-editing resume workbench. The resume itself is the primary interface: users edit the final document in place, Presume keeps it within explicit page and line constraints, and export produces a stable Letter-sized artifact independent of browser zoom. Review is a secondary, advisory workflow that supplies evidence without rewriting or mutating the resume.

Success means the product feels precise and dependable while disappearing behind the document task. Users should understand what is editable, what is saved, what affects formatting, and when review evidence is available without losing ownership of the content.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, document-led.

The quality bar is a flagship portfolio-quality product surface: restrained and professionally appropriate, but visibly intentional enough to feel premium rather than merely functional.

## Anti-references

- Generic clickbait SaaS dashboards or template-heavy shadcn defaults.
- Excessively rounded, soft, glassy, or decorative interfaces.
- Card-heavy layouts where every region receives equal visual weight.
- Marketing-style visual noise, ornamental animation, and oversized controls.
- Resume builders that treat the document as a secondary preview pane.
- Automated-writing experiences that obscure or surrender user control.

## Design Principles

1. **The document is the product surface.** Application chrome supports the fixed resume instead of competing with it.
2. **Precision earns trust.** Alignment, typography, spacing, state language, and export behavior should feel deliberate and deterministic.
3. **Reveal complexity progressively.** Fit and Review remain understandable in compact states and expand only when their detail is useful.
4. **Restraint over decoration.** Premium quality comes from hierarchy, material consistency, and interaction craft—not extra cards, gradients, or motion.
5. **Keep the user in control.** Editing stays direct, review stays advisory, and automated behavior remains transparent and non-mutating.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, semantics, keyboard operation, and focus visibility.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and never rely on motion alone to communicate state.
- Preserve understandable status and warning language without relying on color alone.
- Maintain usable touch targets at the documented narrow-screen boundary.
- Treat narrow layouts as graceful degradation while keeping the desktop document workbench primary.
- Preserve browser-zoom resilience and the fixed-canvas scrolling contract.
