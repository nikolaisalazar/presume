## Production treatment

The approved photograph is preserved as the composition:

- one loose foreground sheet in the lower-left;
- one blank paper stack in the upper-right;
- an uninterrupted upper and central field behind the live hero copy.

A clear, static, anonymized resume hierarchy is printed only onto the loose
foreground sheet. A strong name line, contact line, section labels, aligned
role/date rows, and short bullet marks make the document recognizable without
introducing readable text. The line work is perspective-warped to the
photographed sheet and alpha-composited into the source pixels. The stack,
sheet boundaries, shadows, crop, and negative space are not regenerated. The
artwork contains no real resume or personal data.

The production source is then center-cropped to the existing `14:9` delivery
contract and encoded as:

- `document-horizon-1120.webp` — `1120 × 720`, quality `84`;
- `document-horizon-2200.webp` — `2200 × 1414`, quality `86`.

Light rendering matches the existing `--background` application field; Dark
rendering retains the approved Dark Surround. Both remain controlled by
semantic CSS. No meter, shader, interface layer, or ornamental motion is
present.
