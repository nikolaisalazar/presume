## Deliberately removed or weakened

- Universal authority over all web UI, dashboards, prototypes, and components.
- Mandatory use for small landing fixes and faithful implementation.
- Mandatory conventional section order, FAQ, risk reversal, and conversion climax.
- Universal copy bans such as forbidding all hyphens.
- Any permission to fabricate testimonials, metrics, or placeholder validation.
- Automatic propagation of landing visual rules into the protected product UI.
